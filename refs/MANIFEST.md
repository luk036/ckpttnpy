## Notes

- **Ref 6 title mismatch.** No paper titled *"The Hamilton cycle problem for the
  middle levels graph"* could be found; the citation appears to be a paraphrase.
  The closest (and almost certainly intended) work is P. Gregor, T. Mütze,
  J. Nummenpalo, *"A short proof of the middle levels theorem,"* Discrete
  Analysis, Paper No. 8, 2018 (arXiv:1710.08249). That is the PDF stored as
  `06-gregor-2019-middle-levels.pdf`. The 2018/2019 discrepancy is due to the
  arXiv preprint (Oct 2017) vs. journal publication (May 2018) vs. the 2019
  citation year in the paper.
- **Ref 7 not downloaded.** `Algorithms for VLSI Physical Design Automation` is a
  copyrighted Springer/Kluwer book and no legitimate free copy was reachable.
  archive.org, LibGen, and Anna's Archive are unreachable from this network; the
  vdoc.pub copy was removed for copyright; epdf.pub returned HTTP 403;
  studylib.net is behind a captcha. The book can be obtained from Springer
  (ISBN 978-0-7923-8393-2) or a library. Ref 8 is a different, freely available
  book that covers the same partitioning material.
- **Ref 8 edition.** The stored PDF is the 2011 (1st) edition; the paper cites no
  edition, so this is the edition the DOI/ISBN in the citation corresponds to.
